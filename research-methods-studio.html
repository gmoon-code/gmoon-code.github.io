Academic resource design

Research Methods Studio

A structured research-learning environment designed to keep the reasoning behind methodological decisions visible from early question development through writing.

GitHub repository
Core route

Research question → literature work → methods → data and statistics → writing → review and feedback.

What it supports

Literature Workspace

Search logging, screening, source evaluation, study matrices, theme and disagreement mapping, and claim-to-source tracing.

Methods Lab

Structured method decisions and a pre-collection audit before data collection begins.

Data & Statistics Lab

Local CSV import, data-quality checks, variable and design mapping, analysis, and Results-ready records.

Writing Lab

Evidence maps, section-boundary checks, citation auditing, and whole-paper consistency review.

Design approach

Methodological structure made visible.

The interface uses the same Moon Notes visual system as The Story Method. Times New Roman, white paper surfaces, black ink, thin gray rules, small-radius cards, and the Yellow → Green → Turquoise → Pink sequence help communicate research phases without turning the site into a dashboard.
