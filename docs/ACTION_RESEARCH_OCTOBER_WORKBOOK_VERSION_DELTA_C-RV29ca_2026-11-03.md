## Scientific and ethical implications

The October revisions clarify a later written operational definition of **reported** preparation enactment, but a timestamped prospective rubric, explanation of when that stricter criterion was applied, and evidence of any independent re-adjudication were **not** obtained from the workbook itself. The copied unchanged binary flags do not by themselves demonstrate that a newly written definition was historically applied before analysing the responses. Conversely, nothing in these comparisons demonstrates that the flags are incorrect or that the procedure did not occur.

The original student answers or LMS submissions, dated transformations, administration forms, signed assent/guardian permission, historical school director authorization and separate second-reader adjudication materials remain **not authenticated** by these workbooks. C-RV29ar already established that an available September permission template has blank sign-off fields, without concluding that no authorization ever existed.

The academic site `research-action.html` currently uses the documented de-identified **retained-record paraphrase** wording and states only descriptive comparisons and limits on cross-task causal inference. Its public original Unit 3 case discussion does **not** quote the two changed Unit 2 strings. No headline numerical, original quote or ethics source text correction is supported by the new findings.
