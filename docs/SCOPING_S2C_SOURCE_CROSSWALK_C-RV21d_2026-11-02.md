## The previously missing third original-source schooling-history example

**S01, Magdalena Pando (2024)** provides unusually explicit reporting and was marked `Not reported comparably` in July S2C for each of focal-language condition, instructional placement and prior schooling.

Pando's **Methods / Research design** states that the three selected **Grade 6 emergent bilingual students** were Spanish native speakers with identified English proficiency levels; each had received **dual-language English–Spanish instruction during elementary Grades K–5**, and in Grade 6 they were taught through **English as a Second Language instructional models** consisting of English-medium instruction. The **Instructional context** describes the six-week matter-and-chemical-changes unit, delivered by the researcher in additional short group tutorials, with explicit *content and language learning objectives* and student explanations that could be spoken or written. This paper therefore contains:
- **Explicit prior schooling**, elementary dual-language learning history tied to the same identified participants;
- **Explicit current programme/instructional placement**, English-as-a-Second-Language instructional models in Grade 6 (distinct from merely calling a school English-medium);
- **Identifiable focal language conditions**, tasks/researcher-supported instruction and students' spoken/written science explanations during English-language science learning.

Pando is a **third distinct original-source demonstrated case** of prior-schooling reporting, alongside the original-author descriptions in **S02 Uddling and Reath Warren (2023)** (Spain/UK/Sweden schooling) and **S04 Widing et al. (2022)** (years attended Swedish schools). The count of separately demonstrated included-source examples now equals **3**. **Important limit:** without the August final S2C decisions, this does not prove these were **exactly the three study IDs** counted by the author; the evidence independently supports a defensible **3/20** total and reveals that the July S01 and S02/S04 zero-codes did not carry through to the newer operational approach.

The Pando finding is more decisive for programmatic placement than many other candidates: a **named and applied** ESL instructional model is identified. It is not just a generic intervention added to otherwise unspecified programme placement. Pando also provides direct support for one of the six July focal-language negatives that could have been reclassified in the August 18/20 total.
