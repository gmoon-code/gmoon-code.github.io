It explicitly labels its full text as requiring access.

A second publicly retrievable original research-project PDF at [Project READI](https://projectreadi.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Nasir_Rosebery_Warren_Lee_LearningAsCulturalProcess.pdf) was **opened and page 1 visually inspected as a screenshot**. The PDF is **one page** and gives a prose chapter summary. Its citation, however, calls the work the **2014 second edition while giving pp. 489–504**. That page range belongs to the **earlier edition**, as supported by the first-edition DOI **10.1017/CBO9780511816833.030**, a [University of North Texas original course bibliography](https://guides.library.unt.edu/c.php?g=1344687&p=9917828) identifying the earlier version, and [Tufts course material](https://sites.tufts.edu/ed130mini/identities-2/excerpt-from-learning-as-a-cultural-process-by-nailah-suad-nasir-ann-s-roebery-beth-warren-and-carol-d-lee/) identifying a 2006 489–504 reference. The earlier-edition year is variously catalogued as 2005 or 2006, so do not invent a single exact printing year or mistake the abstract for the substantial original 2014 chapter.

An additional [Penn State course listing](https://courses.worldcampus.psu.edu/welcome/ldt527/001/common/schedule.html) quotes a different 2014-chapter abstract stressing empirical accounts of cultural repertoires across in- and out-of-school settings, which underscores why a first-edition summary is not sufficient to authenticate the exact revised edition's argument in full. The Cambridge 2014 publisher record is authoritative for the site's bibliographic metadata.
