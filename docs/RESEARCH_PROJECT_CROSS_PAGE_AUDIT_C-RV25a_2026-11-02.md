## Cross-page findings

1. **Scoping review evidence tier and scale.** The overview correctly calls it an ERIC-bounded review of **20 included empirical studies**, which matches the separate scoping page. That page reports the initial **216** ERIC screening records and **10 written / 6 classroom talk / 4 models or drawings** dominant evidence classifications in the August preprint. The overview uses general descriptive wording about written, interactional and visual forms rather than silently importing the 28 articles from a *different* published González-Howard synthesis. Its figure caption explicitly distinguishes 28 articles in that source's synthesis from the author's 20 included reports.
2. **Action research scope and treatment.** Both pages report **28 AP Biology students** and a focused comparison of **ten** Unit 3 response pairs after feedback-assisted correction. The overview accurately describes multiple relationships appearing or being omitted and makes clear that subsequent student-specific questions were **suggested after analysis, not administered**. The page does not attribute causal gains to comparisons of different biology units.
3. **Luis and S20 represent different evidentiary circumstances.** The cross-project paragraph describes a bilingual ecological explanation in the included Pierson study and a later post-feedback response in the author's own action research. The linked pages confirm those respective cases. The comparison is explicitly qualified because the task language, teaching/feedback timing and kinds of evidence differ. The overview makes a careful methodological analogy **without claiming the two are equivalent student learning trajectories**.
4. **Different publication statuses remain distinguishable.** Scoping work is labelled **preprint available**, the action study **unpublished manuscript**, and the classification essay **independent scholarly essay**. The current status wording does not claim publication of an unaccepted empirical article or present the essay as an original participant study. Confidential original records and consent/rater provenance remain separately unverified as set out in C-RV22.
5. **Classification essay is not an unacknowledged repetition of action-research findings.** Its cases concern the interpretation of **haemoglobin S/malaria risk** and the history of **XYY behavioural inference**, while the action study examines explanations written in response to three distinct AP Biology mechanisms. The overview's short essay description identifies the essay's particular role and includes a resolved link.

## Style, narrative connections and repetition

The overview's project section introduces the two distinct empirical inquiries before a concrete cross-project question about information obscured when only later answers are considered. It next identifies an empirical boundary that motivates prospective research interests about who can influence classroom judgment. This sequence moves from evidence comparison to an uninvestigated curricular question, rather than representing the prospective question as an observed outcome.

The essay and project pages revisit “what evidence allows someone to infer”, but they apply the idea to different materials. This is **productive thematic continuity** rather than unnecessary same-text duplication. The present paragraphs use relatively varied sentence beginnings and do not require additional stock transitions or inflated concluding claims. The author's restrained and precise voice is preserved.
