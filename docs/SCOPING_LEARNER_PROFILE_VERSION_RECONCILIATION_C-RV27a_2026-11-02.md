## Provenance and access levels

A Project Files search located two prior supplementary-data workbooks, one dated **14 July 2026** and a second dated **16 July 2026**, along with a separate **12 August 2026 manuscript**. The two workbooks were materialized into the private audit runtime and inspected as actual XLSX sheets rather than relying solely on search excerpts. The original source files remain private in the Project/Library and are not copied into the public repository.

The July workbooks each contain a sheet named `S2C Learner Profile`, with headers for study ID, manuscript citation, learner and language labels, focal language condition, instructional placement, newcomer status, prior schooling, biliteracy trajectory and prior disciplinary language exposure. Both have **20 unique study IDs, S01–S20**. The two historical versions' complete **20 × 13 S2C cells** were compared and matched with **zero changed cells**. This is source-authentic *for the older July snapshots*, not proof that they were the final coding version attached to the later August manuscript or preprint.

## Interpretation, limitations and action

The discrepancy is evidence of **different coding snapshots**, not sufficient evidence of an incorrect current published finding. A revised August S2C workbook, recoded study-level evidence or documented changed definitions could explain the additions of four focal language conditions, two instructional placements and three prior-schooling entries. No such newer S2C source record was identified in this bounded Files search, and the study IDs underlying those later additions were **not invented**. Agreement between the later manuscript and website is not independent confirmation of the underlying coding decisions.

**Issue status:** **Blocked pending final-version source record or documented study-by-study recoding**. A completed full-text audit of the 20 cited publications would be needed to reconstruct the newer classifications independently if a final original matrix cannot be obtained, and must be labelled a new recoding rather than preservation of the author's original coding. No replacement of counts, claims, study IDs or other public prose is justified by these two July workbooks.

The current website's research-status language remains appropriately qualified, and no unpublished participant-level records were shared or added to the repository. The separate AP Biology action-research raw response/permission/rater audit was **not** completed in this stage.
