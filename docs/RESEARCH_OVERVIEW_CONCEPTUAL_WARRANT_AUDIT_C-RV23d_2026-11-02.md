## Writing, cohesion, and cross-page quality

The multilingual paragraph moves from researchers' views of language to competing but potentially complementary goals for access and transformation, then to peer critique as one specific opportunity for student epistemic agency. This is a defensible intellectual progression; each original source serves a distinct function, and the brief paragraph does not wrongly imply one single research design. The cultural paragraph treats the Menominee fish task as context-specific evidence and shifts to the additional cultural-epistemological discussion without portraying culture as a deficit. The classroom authority paragraph proceeds from a conceptual limit of predetermined practices to rightful presence and finally poses a prospective practitioner-research question, preserving the author's reflective and humble tone. The sidebar's survey case is correctly situated outside the author's own study.

No unnecessary em-dash/prose-colon/rule-of-three rewriting or generic connective phrases are recommended. Avoid adding original-source sample sizes solely for display, since these would interrupt the authorial flow without improving the specific inferences.
