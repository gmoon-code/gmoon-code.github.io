The study followed a beginning teacher across the first five years of work in an alternative public high school. The website's description of activism and school responsibilities is appropriately bounded.
- **Puvirajah, Verma and Webb (2012)**, *Examining the mediation of power in a collaborative community*, DOI `10.1007/s11422-012-9394-2`. Site describes collaboration and the informal setting without treating it as an ordinary curricular intervention.
