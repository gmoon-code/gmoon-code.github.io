## Scientific fact-checks of originally hypothetical examples

**Sex-development representation.** The opening invites readers to imagine a diagram simplifying a pathway from chromosomes to reproductive anatomy. A hypothetical student asks how differing hormone responses fit in. Original textbook-level clinical developmental synthesis [Ray & Racine, *Endotext*, updated 24 July 2025](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK279001/), together with [clinical 46,XY developmental mechanisms](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279170/), distinguishes embryonic chromosomal constitution and gonadal gene-regulatory mechanisms from later hormone production, receptor-specific effects and tissue differentiation. The example is therefore **scientifically plausible and demonstrably simplified**, and it implicitly asks which relevant causal levels the classroom diagram has omitted. The word “Imagine” correctly identifies it as hypothetical. No living student is asserted to have disclosed their body, identity or medical history.

**Cell-signalling representation.** The second example explicitly says a student **might** ask what happens if a receptor remains active after a signal disappears. The teacher might direct attention to receptor inactivation or downstream pathways. Open original molecular biology reference chapters [*Signaling through Enzyme-Linked Cell-Surface Receptors*](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26822/), [*Signaling through G-Protein-Linked Cell-Surface Receptors*](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26912/) and a recent [2024 review on cell–cell communication and signal termination](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41392-024-01888-z) show different receptor down-regulation, phosphorylation, dephosphorylation, feedback and downstream termination processes. Because the public text **does not identify a specific receptor or assert that all signals terminate by the same pathway**, the example is biologically appropriate. The actual AP Biology teaching case is a **photosynthetic electron-transport assessment**, and the hypothetical cell-signalling lesson is **not** represented as that documented student episode.

**Scientific representational integrity.** The separately audited `#sex-classification` section describes gonadal determination, hormone responses and subsequent anatomical differentiation with reference to original clinical reviews. The independent essay examines HbS/malaria and historical XYY claims, including why a classification cannot by itself establish mechanisms, outcomes or behaviour. Those are distinct arguments, even where both projects return to the question of what a model or category leaves unexplained. None states that physiological processes are merely social conventions.

## Sentence, transition and cross-section audit

**Opening:** Three hypothetical sentences move from the simplified scientific diagram to an omitted mechanism and then to a concrete possible teacher response. The next paragraph locates the teacher's choice within disciplinary/curricular expectations and the social institutions in which scientific explanations are evaluated. The author's justice-oriented research interest emerges from this example rather than entering as a disconnected slogan. It is broad enough to be an intellectual framing and does not claim that a specific local classroom study already tested institutional racism or colonialism.

**Projects to inquiry questions:** Two empirical descriptions are kept distinguishable from each other and from the separate scholarly essay. Their comparative paragraph explicitly says different task and language conditions limit equivalence, while the following sentence identifies a question about classroom judgments that the projects **had not yet investigated**. The paragraph thus marks a research gap rather than embellishing a finding.

**Research interests:** The opening introduces literature on teacher decisions, language/cultural knowledge and modelling, before identifying a particular question about who can influence the criteria for scientific explanation. The receptor example then gives that question a concrete mechanism, and the succeeding paragraph turns to actual scholarship on planning and in-the-moment teacher noticing. Repetition of “contributions” and “questions” in this section is conceptually warranted and does not create a uniformly mechanical sequence.

**Closing section:** The passage starting “Evaluating a scientific explanation and responding to a student's challenge involve different judgments” distinguishes the truth-conditions of a developmental model from the *curricular authority* question of whether a student can change the investigation. The related-writing description appropriately points readers to different HbS and XYY cases without reprising their entire analyses. The invitation to contact the author remains modest and is not promotional filler.

**House style:** Across the Research non-bibliography prose, Canadian English spelling, precise student/teacher actor relationships, genuine variability in transitions and paragraph length, and qualifying phrases remain consistent. No em dash or prose colon was detected in the stripped non-reference copy. A source-title dash, if any, belongs to an original bibliographic title and is not automatically eligible for editing. No fabricated classroom anecdote or causal conclusion emerged.
