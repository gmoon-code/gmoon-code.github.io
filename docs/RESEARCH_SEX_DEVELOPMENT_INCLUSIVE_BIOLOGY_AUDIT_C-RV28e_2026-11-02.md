### Zemenick, Turney, Webster, Jones and Weber (2022) | Complete original publisher text

- The original full [Oxford University Press *BioScience* article](https://academic.oup.com/bioscience/article/72/5/481/6547662) was read in its original publisher-hosted HTML, including the six principles, introductory definitions, topic illustrations, limitations and conclusion.
