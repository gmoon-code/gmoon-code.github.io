- **2014 second edition**, chapter 34, pages **686–706**, DOI `10.1017/CBO9781139519526.041` (**website's correct reference**).
- **2022 third edition**, chapter 29, pages **581–601**, DOI `10.1017/9781108888295.036`.

Those page numbers belong to the **2005** edition, not the 2014 edition. The web PDF text reader exposed only a short project summary and citation record, and an attempt to visually screenshot its page returned an access error. The separately available Scribd copy titled “Chapter 29” belongs to a different edition/version, not automatically the website's chapter 34.

There is no unsupported quantitative learning outcome or transfer from this handbook chapter to a particular classroom. Because complete original **2014 second-edition** chapter prose was not read, **Nasir row 37 remains A**. Citing the 2005/2022 copies as proof that the exact 2014 text was verified would be scholarly source substitution.
