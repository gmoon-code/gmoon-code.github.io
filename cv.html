Scoping Review of Multilingual Learners’ Science Understanding

2026

Preprint · Moon, Y. E. (2026). Evidence routes and validity risk in claims about multilingual learners’ science understanding in secondary science: A scoping review [Preprint]. https://doi.org/10.35542/osf.io/kznvj_v1

  • Examined how research on multilingual secondary science learning supports claims about students’ scientific understanding and how those claims depend on the forms of evidence collected.
  • Compared researchers’ uses of classroom talk, writing, diagrams, models, gesture, and movement between languages when interpreting students’ scientific ideas.
  • Developed a diagnostic framework considering evidence forms, disciplinary targets, learner conditions, and interpretive procedures in published studies.

Classroom Action Research in Biology

2026

Unpublished manuscript · Reconsidering assessment evidence and practitioner judgment through classroom action research in biology. Prepared for Educational Action Research.

  • Examined two adjustments to post-assessment correction in one AP Biology class of 28 students, using written explanations, feedback reflections, preparation records, and practitioner notes.
  • Compared ten paired responses to the same photosynthesis mechanism and identified differences concealed by later completeness classifications.
  • Traced how evaluating reconstruction informed more specific questions for subsequent assessment while leaving the adjustment’s effects on understanding unresolved.
