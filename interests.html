Interests

Outside teaching and research, I spend much of my time with pungmul, books, and media.

Pungmul

I play in a queer pungmul ensemble (풍물패). We held a concert in 2024, participate in the Seoul Pride parade each year, and busk in support of queer rights.

Books and media

A few books, series, and shows I enjoy and return to.

Books

The Priory of the Orange Tree

Samantha Shannon · Fantasy

I've always enjoyed stories with worlds that feel as though they extend far beyond the pages. I loved getting to know the characters, their different beliefs, and the ways they slowly come to understand one another. And, of course, the dragons and the lovely queer romance made it even more special to me.

The Lord of the Rings

J. R. R. Tolkien · Fantasy

There's something about this story that always makes me want to return to it. I think it's the kindness and loyalty between the characters, especially in the little moments when they look after one another. I also enjoy wandering through Tolkien's wonderfully detailed world, even when the journey takes its time.

I'm Waiting for You

Kim Bo-young · Science fiction

I have a soft spot for science fiction that makes me think about ordinary human feelings in unfamiliar circumstances. The stories are imaginative and sometimes quite sad, but there's a tenderness to them that I really appreciate. I especially liked the way time and distance become part of what it means to care for someone.

Rose

Li-Young Lee · Poetry

I like how gently these poems approach memories of family, love, and growing up. Some of the images seem so ordinary at first, but they stay with me in ways I don't always know how to explain. Like Lee, I grew up with a father who was a pastor, and I think my own journey of questioning and deconstructing faith makes some of these poems feel a little closer to me. It's a collection I enjoy returning to slowly, a few poems at a time.

Shows

Detective Conan

Anime · Mystery

This series has a special place in my heart. I still enjoy trying to solve the mysteries alongside Conan, although I'm usually a few steps behind him. I think I've also grown quite attached to the characters over time. There's something comforting about spending time with them again, even when another murder mystery is waiting around the corner.

Dimension 20

Actual-play · Fantasy · Comedy

I really enjoy watching people imagine and create stories together. The cast makes me laugh a lot, but I also appreciate how much care they put into their characters and the worlds they share. Some of my favourite moments are the unexpected little exchanges that turn into something surprisingly touching.
