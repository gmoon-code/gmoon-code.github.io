Outside teaching and research

Music, climbing, and reading are regular parts of my life outside work. I wanted this page to hold those parts of my life without turning them into another professional statement.

Pungmul

I am a member of a pungmul ensemble (풍물패). I value the collective character of the practice, where percussion, movement, timing, and attention to the rest of the group all develop together.

Indoor climbing

I climb indoors regularly. I enjoy working through movement problems over repeated attempts, especially when a route asks for balance, coordination, or a different way of using the same holds.

Reading

Books are another constant part of my life. I want this section to remain selective, with books I have genuinely enjoyed, returned to, or kept thinking about after reading them, rather than becoming a complete reading log.
