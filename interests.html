Interests

Pungmul

I joined a queer pungmul (풍물) ensemble because I wanted to learn traditional Korean music and find people with whom I could play it. I love the force of the drums and gongs and the way a performance develops through everyone listening and responding to one another. Learning more about the music has also introduced me to histories that extend far beyond our own group. UNESCO's account of nongak (opens in a new tab) describes its place in communal gatherings, while research on the pungmul movement (opens in a new tab) examines its relationship with labour, farmers' organizing, and South Korea's democratisation movement during the 1980s. I feel grateful to participate in one small part of this living tradition.

Our ensemble gave a formal concert in 2024, and we participate in the Seoul Pride parade every year. We also give seasonal busking and smaller performances in support of queer rights. Playing together in public has given me a way to take part in Korean cultural traditions alongside people with whom I feel at home. Our presence also has a meaning beyond the music in a country where LGBTQ rights and access to public space remain contested. I am glad that we can be heard there, together.

Books and media

Books

The Hobbit

J. R. R. Tolkien · Fantasy

The Shire is what I picture first when I think of The Hobbit. I love its green hills and slower pace, and the sense of a life closely intertwined with nature. Tolkien's descriptions make me want to linger over the countryside even as Bilbo ventures farther from it. I can imagine landscapes and paths so different from my everyday world that reading becomes a kind of journey of its own. I am fond of Bilbo, too, especially his kindness and the ways he changes without losing it.

I'm Waiting for You

Kim Bo-young · Science fiction

I love how much hope persists in I'm Waiting for You. Plans change, circumstances become difficult, and other people question the choices being made, yet the desire to meet remains. I find something deeply moving in the willingness to wait and keep making opportunities for love. Kim Bo-young's scientific imagination carries these feelings through extraordinary changes in time and distance, while the longing itself feels familiar. My favourite is the final story, “On My Way to You.” More than anything, I love its affection for love itself. I love love, and I love seeing it cherished so openly.

Rose

Li-Young Lee · Poetry

I return to Li-Young Lee's poems for their rhythms and the vivid images and emotions carried by the sound of his language. “From Blossoms” is my favourite. I love the way the lines move and how much joy they hold in an ordinary sensory experience. “Persimmons” stays with me for another reason. Its attention to language, taste, and family relationships reminds me of translating English for my family when I was growing up. Both poems make me want to linger over individual lines, enjoying how the words sound as much as what they bring to mind.

Shows

Detective Conan

Anime · Mystery

I keep returning to Detective Conan for the way its characters take part in solving problems. I enjoy noticing what each person contributes and how their different roles and relationships come together during a case. Conan is especially delightful to follow because he sees connections I would never have made myself. My favourite character is Dr. Agasa, the jolly inventor who looks after Conan and the children with such kindness. His care for them is one of the reasons I find the series so comforting, even amid its mysteries.

Dimension 20

Actual-play · Fantasy · Comedy

I enjoy how Dimension 20 starts with a recognizable world and a set of themes, then leaves room for the players to take the story somewhere unexpected. Their decisions can change the direction of a campaign, and I love watching characters develop through those surprises. The diversity of the characters and the ways their different perspectives enter the storytelling make these worlds especially enjoyable to me. Above all, I love the cooperation. Each person contributes something the others can respond to, and the story becomes more interesting through what they create together.
