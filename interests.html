Interests

Pungmul

I joined a queer pungmul (풍물) ensemble because I wanted to learn traditional Korean music and find people with whom I could play it. I love the force of the drums and gongs and the way a performance develops through everyone listening and responding to one another. Learning more about the music has also introduced me to histories that extend far beyond our own group. UNESCO's account of nongak (opens in a new tab) describes its place in communal gatherings, while research on the pungmul movement (opens in a new tab) examines its relationship with labour, farmers' organizing, and South Korea's democratisation movement during the 1980s. I feel grateful to participate in one small part of this living tradition.

Our ensemble gave a formal concert in 2024, and we participate in the Seoul Pride parade every year. We also give seasonal busking and smaller performances in support of queer rights. Playing together in public has given me a way to take part in Korean cultural traditions alongside people with whom I feel at home. Our presence also has a meaning beyond the music in a country where LGBTQ rights and access to public space remain contested. I am glad that we can be heard there, together.

Books and media

Books

The Hobbit

J. R. R. Tolkien · Fantasy

Whenever I think of The Hobbit, I picture the Shire first. I love its green hills, the slower pace of life, and the feeling of being closely connected to the surrounding countryside. Tolkien makes those landscapes so vivid that I find myself imagining what it would be like to wander through them. As Bilbo travels farther from home, that pleasure of exploring another world stays with me. I also love his kindness, especially the way he changes through the journey without losing that part of himself.

I'm Waiting for You

Kim Bo-young · Science fiction

I love the hope that runs through I'm Waiting for You, especially when plans change without warning and the way forward becomes difficult. Even when others question their choices, the characters keep waiting and finding ways to meet. Kim Bo-young's imagined science can put extraordinary stretches of time and distance between people, while their emotions remain so easy to understand. I find that persistence very moving, particularly in “On My Way to You,” the final story and my favourite. I love love, and I am glad to read something that cherishes it so openly.

Rose

Li-Young Lee · Poetry

I return to Li-Young Lee's poems for the rhythm of his lines and the vivid images and emotions they carry. “From Blossoms” is my favourite, especially for the joy I hear in the movement of the words. I love how it brings an ordinary sensory experience so vividly to life. That attention to the senses also makes me think of “Persimmons,” where taste becomes bound up with words and family. Having grown up translating English for my family, I feel a special affection for that poem. Both have a way of making me slow down and listen to the words as I read.

Shows

Detective Conan

Anime · Mystery

Watching Detective Conan, I especially enjoy seeing how each person contributes to solving a mystery. Conan makes connections between clues that I would never have put together myself, and following his reasoning is always a pleasure. The cases also bring together characters I have grown fond of, particularly Dr. Agasa. I love his cheerful curiosity as a scientist and inventor, along with the kindness he shows Conan and the children. Spending time with him and the other familiar characters makes the show a comforting one for me.

Dimension 20

Actual-play · Fantasy · Comedy

I love how Dimension 20 can surprise me even when I recognise the themes a campaign is built around. The world gives the story a sense of direction, yet the players' choices can still lead it somewhere I never expected. I enjoy watching the characters grow through those turns, especially when their different personalities and experiences lead them to respond to the same situation in their own ways. One player's decision might give another a new possibility to explore, and I love seeing what the cast creates together from those exchanges.
