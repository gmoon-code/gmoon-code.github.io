Interests

Pungmul

I joined a queer pungmul (풍물) ensemble because I wanted to learn traditional Korean music and find people with whom I could play it. I love the force of the drums and gongs and the way a performance develops as everyone listens and responds to one another. Learning more about the music has also introduced me to histories beyond our group, from communal gatherings to performances associated with South Korea's democracy movement. I am grateful to have a small place in this living tradition.

Our ensemble gave a formal concert in 2024, and we participate in the Seoul Pride parade every year. We also busk seasonally and perform at smaller events supporting queer rights. Through these performances, I have found a way to share a Korean cultural tradition with people among whom I feel at home. In a country where LGBTQ rights remain contested, playing in public carries a significance beyond the music. I am glad that we can be heard there, together.

Books and media

Books

The Hobbit

J. R. R. Tolkien · Fantasy

The Shire is what I picture first when I think of The Hobbit. Its green hills and quiet countryside suggest a pace of life closely connected to nature. Tolkien's descriptions make those places vivid enough that I can imagine travelling far beyond my everyday surroundings. I enjoy the adventure as Bilbo encounters unfamiliar landscapes and people, gradually finding courage he did not know he had. What stays with me most, though, is his kindness and thoughtfulness, even as the journey changes him.

I'm Waiting for You

Kim Bo-young · Science fiction

What moves me in I'm Waiting for You is how the desire to meet survives sudden changes in plans, difficult circumstances, and criticism from others. Even after their carefully made plans unravel, the letters keep returning to the possibility of meeting again. Kim Bo-young's scientific imagination lets these feelings unfold across extraordinary experiences of time and distance, while the hope and longing remain easy to understand. The final story, “On My Way to You,” is my favourite. I have always loved stories that celebrate love, and I especially appreciate how openly and tenderly this one does so.

Rose

Li-Young Lee · Poetry

I return to Li-Young Lee's poems for the rhythm of his lines and the vivid images and emotions they carry. “From Blossoms” is my favourite, especially for the movement of its words and the joy I hear in them. I am also fond of “Persimmons” and the connections Lee traces among language, memory, and family. Growing up, I often helped my parents navigate English while still learning the language myself. Parts of the poem remind me of how difficult it can be to carry meaning between languages, even when the words themselves seem simple.

Shows

Detective Conan

Anime · Mystery

I enjoy Detective Conan for the different kinds of knowledge its characters bring to solving a mystery. Conan notices connections I would have missed, and following how he works through the clues is part of the pleasure. Dr. Agasa is my favourite character. His cheerfulness and care for Conan and the children make him especially endearing to me. I like that a scientist and inventor can bring so much warmth to the people around him.

Dimension 20

Actual-play · Fantasy · Comedy

One of the things I love about Dimension 20 is that I can recognise the themes of a campaign and still have no idea where the story will go. The players build it together, so an unexpected decision or exchange can change what happens next and how the characters develop. I enjoy the diversity of the characters and the different perspectives the cast brings to each situation. I especially enjoy their exchanges around the table, where the cast can make space for a serious moment and then surprise one another with something funny.
