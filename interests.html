Interests

Outside teaching and research, I spend much of my time with pungmul, indoor climbing, books, and media.

Pungmul

I play in a queer pungmul ensemble (풍물패). We held a concert in 2024, participate in the Seoul Pride parade each year, and busk in support of queer rights.

Indoor climbing

I climb indoors for both the exercise and the puzzles. I enjoy working out the sequence of a route, trying it, and adjusting how I move until the problem makes sense.
