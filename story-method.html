Projects / The Story Method

The Story Method

A multi-course science learning platform built around narrative journeys, scientific representations, retrieval, review, and challenge structures.

Open live site
Current structure

Course → Unit → Journey → Scene → memory objects, concepts, equations, calculations, graphs, diagrams, data tables, particulate models, vectors, lab contexts, review, and challenge work.

Why I built it

I wanted a way to place scientific relationships inside a larger structure that students could revisit, instead of presenting each concept as an isolated block of information. The project grew into both a student-facing learning environment and a teacher Content Studio.

What I designed

Student experience

Courses, units, journeys, and scenes that connect narrative progression with scientific concepts, questions, review, and challenge tasks.

Content Studio

A teacher-facing environment for organizing course packages, units, journeys, scenes, concepts, question banks, review targets, and challenge content.

Science representations

A generic component system supporting concepts, equations, calculations, graphs, diagrams, data tables, particulate models, vectors, and laboratory contexts.

Design approach

Academic notebook, not generic learning software.

The visual system uses Times New Roman, warm white paper, white cards, thin gray borders, very small radii, restrained black and white controls, and a Yellow → Green → Turquoise → Pink highlighter progression. Color communicates organization rather than decoration.
