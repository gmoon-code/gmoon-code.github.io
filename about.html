A little about me

I grew up and studied in Canada and now live in South Korea, where I teach science. My background is in biology and biochemistry, though much of my day-to-day life now involves preparing lessons, talking through ideas, and building resources that someone else might find useful.

My training began with the sciences themselves. Teaching and building learning resources have given me more reasons to think about how an explanation or a tool feels to the person trying to use it.

Making things carefully

When I'm preparing a lesson or building a web tool, I spend time on the steps someone will need to follow. A question may ask students to compare results before giving them enough information to make the comparison. A button may use a word that sounds familiar to me but is unclear to someone opening the tool for the first time.

I enjoy working between science, writing, and coding. A small change in wording or layout is sometimes enough to make an activity easier to follow. Other times I need to reconsider the activity itself, and I'm glad the materials can keep changing as I learn from using them.

Working things through together

I appreciate conversations where we can ask simple questions and take the time to answer them. What is this part meant to do? Have we understood the problem in the same way? What might we have missed? I value working with people who are willing to revisit an idea together, including when the first plan needs changing.
