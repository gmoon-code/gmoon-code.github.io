A little about me

I grew up in Canada in an immigrant family. My interest in living systems led me to study biology and biochemistry at the University of British Columbia, and I now teach Advanced Placement Biology and Chemistry in South Korea.

Family and education

As the eldest daughter in my family, I took on responsibilities early. My parents spoke little English, so I often translated documents and helped them navigate institutions whose rules I was learning myself. Alongside those responsibilities, I worked to pay for my education. I remember how difficult it could be to understand what an institution expected when its procedures were unfamiliar and little guidance was available. That experience continues to influence how I think about access to education, especially the assumptions teachers and institutions make about what students and families already know.

Building learning tools

Alongside classroom teaching, I develop browser-based learning tools. The Story Method uses fictional journeys to introduce AP Biology questions that students revisit through review and application. Research Methods Studio gives students a place to plan investigations and reconsider earlier decisions as their work develops. Their individual pages describe the current applications and the choices behind them.

Science education and identity

My father was a pastor at the small church I attended as a child. As I came to understand my queer identity and reconsider the beliefs I had inherited, I became more aware of how little another person may know about someone's life. I carry that caution into discussions of human variation in biology, where a question about a scientific category should never be taken as a request for someone to explain their identity. I want students to be able to examine the evidence behind a classification and consider its consequences while retaining control over what they share about themselves. My research page develops some of the questions I am still learning to ask.

Outside school

Outside school, I play pungmul with a queer ensemble. I also enjoy poetry, fantasy, science fiction, mysteries, and collaborative storytelling. The Interests page is a more personal collection of performances, books, and shows that I have wanted to share.
