A little about me

I'm Ye Eun Moon. I grew up and studied in Canada and now live and teach in South Korea. My background is in biology and biochemistry, and I have found that I enjoy the everyday work of helping students ask questions, try explanations, and look closely at the world around them.

Hello

Thank you for visiting. This site brings together some of my work as a teacher, the questions I am beginning to research, and the interests I enjoy outside school.

My path into science education

I studied Biology with a minor in Biochemistry at the University of British Columbia. Since then, I have taught and tutored science in Canada and South Korea. I am currently completing a Master of Education in Science Education at the American College of Education.

Teaching has given me plenty of reasons to reconsider my own explanations. I like the moments when a student asks something I had not anticipated and we have to examine the idea together. I hope to keep approaching those moments with curiosity and care.
