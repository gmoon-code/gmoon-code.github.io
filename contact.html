A little about me

I grew up in Canada in an immigrant family and fell in love with science during my studies. 
I now teach secondary science at an international school in South Korea. My students bring varied languages, cultural experiences, and identities into classrooms where assumptions about sameness and belonging can shape everyday interactions. Teaching them has deepened my interest in how schools respond to difference, whose questions and explanations receive attention, and how science education can offer more inclusive and equitable opportunities to participate.

I've shared more about the work that interests me on my Research and Teaching pages.
