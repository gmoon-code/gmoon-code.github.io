Curriculum and learning resources

I enjoy making materials for the parts of learning that need time, structure, and another opportunity to try. The two projects here grew out of different classroom needs. One helps students revisit scientific ideas through a connected story. The other helps students keep track of their decisions as they develop a research project.

Explore the resources

Both projects have public websites you can explore. I continue to revise them as I notice places where the guidance, scientific content, or sequence of work needs to be clearer.

Research Methods Studio

Students often need support connecting their research question to sources, measurements, analysis, and the claims they eventually write. This resource breaks those decisions into smaller tasks and helps students keep an account of what they chose and why.

What guides the design

I try to leave students with something they can examine and return to, whether that is a diagram, an explanation, a research decision, or a record of feedback. The resources are still developing. Working on them has been another way for me to notice where I need to explain an idea more carefully, make a task easier to navigate, or leave more room for students' own choices.
