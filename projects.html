Projects

These are two learning environments I've built for students to explore independently. Each gives learners a way to return to an earlier idea or decision. You can open both projects in your browser and see the material directly.

AP Biology · Eight released units

The Story Method

Follow a guided journey through scientific scenes and revisit what you remember. The current course includes location-based learning, optional recall prompts, a Review area, and application questions in Challenge Lab.

Try it

Open Unit 1, choose a journey, and visit one location. Notice how the scene, scientific actors, and memory anchors are presented.

Research Methods Studio

Begin with a research interest and work toward a question, evidence base, study design, analysis, and a written report. The public workspace includes guidance, examples, a research snapshot, and tools for revisiting earlier work.

Start with a research interest. Then look at how the proposed question connects with a research approach and the evidence you could collect.
