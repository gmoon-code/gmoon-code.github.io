Projects

Some of the questions I encounter in teaching continue after a lesson ends. Students may need another way to revisit a biological explanation, while an independent research project may require a record of decisions that change as the evidence develops. I have built two browser-based tools for these different kinds of work. The examples below show their instructional design, with screenshots and live previews of the published applications.

The Story Method

Across eight AP Biology units, fictional journeys introduce scientific problems that students encounter again through recall and application. The Unit 1 example begins with an unexpected reporter-cell signal and asks which experimental comparisons could help explain the observed difference.

Research Methods Studio

The eighteen-stage workspace helps students revisit choices from their research question through analysis and writing. Its synthetic radish study follows 90 seeds nested within 18 Petri dishes. Because treatments are assigned to dishes, the example asks why the analysis cannot treat every seed as an independently assigned experimental unit.
