Projects

These two browser-based tools grew from different questions in my teaching. One uses fictional journeys to bring AP Biology concepts into recall and application activities. The other helps students examine methodological choices as a research project develops. Each project page includes an example of its instructional design and a live preview of the released tool.

AP Biology · Eight released units

The Story Method

Across eight AP Biology units, fictional locations give students a place to encounter scientific problems before returning to them through recall, review, and application. The Unit 1 example follows an unexpected laboratory observation into decisions about experimental controls.

Research Methods Studio

The eighteen-stage workspace supports research planning, literature work, design, analysis, and writing. Its seedling experiment illustrates how identifying the experimental unit can change an entire investigation.
