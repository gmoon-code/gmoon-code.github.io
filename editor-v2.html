Free placement on this screen

Click text or an image to select it. Alt-click inside a card or section to select the whole container. Drag to move with alignment guides (hold Alt while dragging to disable snapping), or use resize handles. Enable text rewrapping below when you want to change line breaks.

When enabled, resize handles change the text box and its line breaks. Its height reserves space in the page. If the text becomes taller, it may overlap content below.

Fitting the height deliberately makes room for the whole paragraph and can move following sections down. Undo is available.

Positioning is saved independently for desktop, tablet and phone and included only in draft previews or staging.
