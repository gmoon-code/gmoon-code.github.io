Something I'm making

Research Methods Studio · In development

Giving student research room to develop

A student may have a question they care about before they know what evidence could answer it. That gap can be difficult to notice once a research paper is taking shape. I'm building Research Methods Studio to help students examine their questions, methods, data, and developing claims together, and return to earlier decisions when a study changes.

See how the workspace is organized →

A useful check for a research plan

  1. What am I trying to find out?
  2. What will my measurements actually tell me?
  3. How far can the evidence take my conclusion?

I am also developing The Story Method, which connects AP Biology ideas through scenes, questions, and scientific representations.
