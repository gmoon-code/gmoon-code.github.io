Science teaching, research, and things I make

Ye Eun Moon

Hello, I'm Ye Eun. I teach biology and chemistry in South Korea, where students' questions often draw my attention to relationships I had assumed were clear. I enjoy following those questions with them, whether we are tracing a biological process or trying to understand what a set of measurements can tell us.

This website gathers some of the teaching materials, research questions, and learning tools that have grown from that work. I have also made room for the music and stories I enjoy outside school. Thank you for visiting.

Teaching

Questions I am investigating

Research

I am interested in whose questions influence the scientific work of a classroom and how teachers interpret reasoning expressed across languages and representations. My Research page develops those concerns through scholarship on participation and biological classification, alongside accounts of two inquiries I have undertaken.

Research Methods Studio

Research Methods Studio follows a project from its initial question through research design, analysis, and writing. At each stage, students can reconsider earlier choices as they discover what their sources, measurements, or methods can actually support. The workspace is available to explore here.

Questions to revisit during a study

  1. What am I trying to find out?
  2. What will my measurements actually tell me?
  3. How far can the evidence take my conclusion?
